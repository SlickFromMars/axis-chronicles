DREAM ACCOUNT

Oliver

The following account was reconstructed by Quill from statements made by Oliver the day following his resurrection.

The accuracy of the account cannot be verified.

So it's the little girl again, the one I told you about before.

This time we weren't on the beach with the two swings, though. I think we were in her room? It looked really strange. The walls seemed to be made of some monochrome plaster. Um... or maybe concrete, I'm not quite sure. When the dream started, I was standing outside of her doorway, and she was nowhere to be seen inside. It felt almost bleak, with dim light coming in through the rounded rectangular window. It must've been close to nighttime. The floor was littered with toys, and she had one of those beds with curtains that draped around it. It almost felt like the room had been decorated to distract her from how dreary the atmosphere was in there.

Afterwards, I went into the room, and I was IMMEDIATELY tackled by her from behind the door. Scared the shit out of me, to be honest, and I went tumbling down to the ground. But then she started tickling me. In my mind, I wanted nothing to do with this, as it's obviously, like, really freaky. That's the thing, though. It was like one of those dreams where you're just watching rather than being an active participant.

That whole thing makes no sense, especially if it has anything to do with the evil necromancy book. I don't see any reason for an evil book to want to have a tickle fight. Honestly, I played along, and we had a tickle fight.

Usually, I wouldn't remember something like that, but we did that for some time. She's very feisty, for sure. Afterwards, she showed me some of her toys and other knick-knacks. Honestly, it made me kind of wish that I had a room like that when I was her age. It felt like I was maybe there for a long time, which is weird because we hardly had any time to do anything. I felt weirdly safe. Almost at home.

Suddenly, I remember is hearing an indistinct, deep voice from down the hall. I couldn't tell what he was saying at all, but she seemed to understand him perfectly because she shot her head up quickly. She stood up and went to the door. I couldn't see who it was or what was happening, and the dream ended shortly after that.

OBSERVATION

Oliver reportedly began laughing during the tickling portion of the dream.

When questioned about this, he was completely unaware.
